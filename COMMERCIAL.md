## What counts as "noncommercial" under PolyForm NC 1.0.0

Per Sections "Noncommercial Purposes", "Personal Uses", and "Noncommercial Organizations" of the license, the following are explicitly permitted without separate permission:

- Academic research and publication

## What counts as "commercial" (requires license)

Any use not within the above, including but not limited to:

- Integrating KDF into a commercial product or SaaS offering
- Providing paid services that internally rely on KDF
- Production deployment of KDF for revenue-generating operations
- Distribution of KDF (modified or unmodified) as part of a commercial software bundle
- Hosted API offerings based on KDF

## FAQ

**Q: I want to run KDF on my laptop for a hobby project. Do I need a license?**
A: No. Personal hobby use is explicitly covered by the PolyForm NC license. Proceed freely.

**Q: I'm a PhD student. Can I use KDF in my thesis research?**
A: Yes. Academic research at a university is noncommercial use and requires no separate license. Please cite the preprint.

**Q: My company wants to evaluate KDF internally. Do we need a commercial license?**
A: Internal nonproduction evaluation is permitted under "Noncommercial Organizations" if no commercial application is anticipated. Once you plan to move toward production use or commercial offering, contact us.

**Q: Can I publish benchmark results comparing KDF to other algorithms?**
A: Yes. Benchmark publication is academic/research use. Please cite the preprint.

**Q: Can I fork the repository and modify the code?**
A: Yes, under PolyForm NC for noncommercial use. For commercial use, the commercial license covers modifications as well.

**Q: What happens after JPO auto-publishes the patent in 2027?**
A: The patent itself becomes publicly viewable, but licensing terms for commercial use remain unchanged. The open-source license of the code (PolyForm NC) is independent of patent publication.
